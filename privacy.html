Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how instabot_megfo ("we", "our", or "the bot") handles information when people interact with our Instagram messaging service.

1. Information We Process

When someone sends a message to our Instagram professional account, the service may process the sender's Instagram-scoped ID, message text, and information included in the webhook event. This information is used to receive messages and generate automated replies.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the messaging bot, respond to incoming messages, troubleshoot errors, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. Storage and Retention

The service is designed to process incoming messages to provide replies. Technical logs may be generated for troubleshooting. Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy and applicable legal requirements. We do not claim that information is never stored by the hosting provider or Meta.

4. Sharing of Information

The service uses Meta's Instagram platform and Cloudflare hosting infrastructure. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to deliver the service and under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the service and its configuration. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You can stop interacting with the bot at any time by no longer messaging the Instagram account. You may also contact us to ask about information associated with your interaction.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been handled improperly, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator through the Instagram account associated with instabot_megfo.

This policy describes the intended operation of the service. It should be updated if actual data collection, storage, sharing, or contact details change.
